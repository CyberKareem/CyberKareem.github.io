Solidity/EVM Smart Contract Security Readiness Review

Review an agreed Solidity/EVM code snapshot for implementation and business-logic weaknesses, reproduce validated issues in an isolated test environment, and prepare remediation before deployment or a formal protocol audit.

  • Solidity/EVM contracts, roles, trust boundaries, and invariants
  • Reentrancy, access control, and external call or delegatecall paths
  • Oracle, flash-loan, frontrunning, and governance attack paths

Best fit
Teams preparing a bounded Solidity/EVM release, reviewing defined modules, or seeking a focused second review alongside a mature audit program.

Relevant background and limits

How an engagement works

Four steps from scope to verification.

  1. 01

    Agree the scope

    Confirm ownership, objectives, targets, exclusions, access, communication, stop conditions, and permitted techniques in writing.

  2. 02

    Assess

    Combine manual testing with targeted tooling while minimizing service and data impact.

  3. 03

    Report

    Deliver validated findings, reproducible evidence, severity reasoning, and prioritized remediation guidance.

  4. 04

    Review and verify

    Walk through the results and record the status of agreed fixes selected for retesting.

Authorization and safety

Testing begins only after written authorization.

Ownership, targets, exclusions, access, safety limits, deliverables, and retest terms are agreed before work begins. Sensitive target details move to a private channel after the initial enquiry.
