TemplateProfessional opportunityTemplateSecurity assessmentTemplateResponsible disclosure

Professional opportunity

Recruitment, employment, speaking, collaboration, or another professional conversation.

Include your organization, the opportunity, location or working arrangement, and a public job description when available.

Security assessment

An authorized assessment, scoped review, or security-consulting enquiry.

Share the high-level asset type, approximate scope, desired outcome, timing, and authorization owner. Do not send targets, credentials, source code, personal data, or vulnerability evidence yet.

Responsible disclosure

Request a private channel for a vulnerability affecting a project or product connected to me.

Ordinary email is not a confidential report intake. Do not include exploit code, credentials, personal data, or unpatched technical details in the first message.
